Our Story

A small bakery with an old-fashioned promise

Crumb & Craft started in 2019 as a two-person kitchen in Sector 35, Chandigarh. Our founder, trained in traditional European baking, believed the city deserved bread with real flavour — long fermentation, stone-milled flour, and butter you can actually taste.

Seven years later we still bake in small batches through the night, so everything you buy in the morning was shaped by hand just hours earlier.

  • ✓No preservatives, ever. If it can't stay fresh naturally, we don't bake it.
  • ✓Local ingredients first. Flour, dairy and fruit sourced from Punjab farms wherever possible.
  • ✓Custom celebration cakes. Birthdays, weddings and corporate orders with 48-hour notice.

Why it matters
